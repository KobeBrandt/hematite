Book a repair café visit

Bring a broken lamp, bike, toaster or jumper and fix it together with a volunteer. Use this service to book a 30-minute slot.

You can book a visit if:

  • you live in the municipality
  • the item fits on a table and you can carry it
  • it is not a gas appliance or a microwave

It takes about 5 minutes. The visit is free; you pay only for spare parts.

Start now

Before you start

You need the make and model of the item, if you know it, and a phone number or email address so we can confirm your slot.

Bring your own spare parts if you already have them. Volunteers bring tools, not stock.
