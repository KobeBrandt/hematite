What we can repair

In short

  • Small electrical items, bikes, clothes and furniture.
  • No gas appliances, microwaves or phone screens.
  • Free visit; you pay for spare parts.

Items we repair

Most things that fit on a table. Volunteers have tools for electrics, textiles, wood and bikes.

Electrical safety

! WarningNever plug in an item that smells of burning. Bring it unplugged.

Half of what comes in only needs a new fuse or a loose wire tightened.

Mina, volunteer electrician

Questions

Can I leave my item and come back later?

No. You fix it together with a volunteer, so you stay for the visit.

What if it cannot be repaired?

The volunteer tells you why and where to recycle it.

Do I need to bring tools?

No. Bring spare parts if you already have them.
