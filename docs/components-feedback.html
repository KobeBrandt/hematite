Feedback

Say what happened and what is happening. A word carries the meaning; colour repeats it. css/components/feedback.css

Badge Atom components/feedback.css

Draft Beta New Submitted Paid Overdue Failed

Use

  • A short status word next to the thing it describes.
  • The word carries the meaning; colour only repeats it.

Don't

  • Make badges clickable. Use a button or link.
  • Use a colour dot without a word.

Alert Molecule components/feedback.css

Info

Bookings for December open on 1 November.

Done

Your photo was uploaded.

Warning

Your booking has no contact phone number yet.

Error

The file is larger than 10 MB. Compress it or choose another photo.

Use

  • role="status" for messages after an action (4.1.3).
  • role="alert" only for errors that need attention now.

Don't

  • Auto-dismiss alerts on a timer (2.2.1).
  • Drop the label word.

Notification banner Organism components/feedback.css

Important

The café is closed on 26 December.

Bookings for that day were moved to 2 January.

Success

Your changes were saved.

Use

  • Top of <main>, above the h1, one per page.
  • For the result of the last action, or news that affects the whole page.

Don't

  • Use it for errors in a form: that is the error summary.
  • Stack several banners.

Warning text Molecule components/feedback.css

! WarningYou can be fined up to €200 if you do not cancel a slot you cannot use.

Use

  • For legal or money consequences people must not miss.
  • Start with visually hidden "Warning".

Don't

  • Use it for tips or general information; use inset text.

Loader, skeleton and progress Atom components/feedback.css

Loading bookings

3 of 5
Preparing

Use

  • Always with words ("Loading bookings"), in a polite live region.
  • aria-busy="true" on the region that is loading; skeletons are aria-hidden.
  • Native <progress>; no value means indeterminate.

Don't

  • Show a spinner for less than a second; it flickers.
  • Use a skeleton when you can show the real content just as fast.

Motion

  • The dotted ring turns one dot at a time, like the ring on the mouse.
  • Everything stops under reduced motion; the words still say what is happening.

Toast Molecule components/feedback.css

Use

  • Short confirmation of a small action: "Copied", "Saved".
  • Contour.toast("Saved") creates the live region for you.

Don't

  • Put the only way to undo something in a toast.
  • Remove it in under 10 seconds (2.2.1).

  • Leave it up for years.
